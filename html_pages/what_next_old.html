Activity that could happen from SEA LIFE over the coming years

Understanding ecosystem responses

Focus: How marine life and ecosystems react to the trials.

  • Marine impact studies: Looking for effects on a wide range of species in tanks at SEA LIFE Weymouth.
  • Mesocosm experiments: Using floating containers to mimic ocean conditions.
  • Marine ecosystem surveys: Baseline and during-trial checks for unintended consequences.

Testing and Improving the Technology

Focus: How the approach works and how we refine it.

  • Ocean alkalinity enhancement trials: Exploring a similar but different approach, where the carbon storage potential of seawater is increased (see longer description below).
  • Technology iteration: Developing technology in a lab then trialling at SEA LIFE.
  • Scaling up: Increasing water flow and performing larger trials.
  • Boat-based trials: Short-term releases to simulate large scale operation.

Tracking What Happens

Focus: Verify effectiveness of carbon removal.

  • Multi-season trials: Performing 12 month or longer experiments to see changes across seasons.
  • Tracer studies: Following released seawater using visible or invisible 'dyes' in water.
  • Data collection: Using boats, buoys, and autonomous vehicles to gather detailed information on water composition and movement.
  • Boat-based trials: Short-term releases to simulate large scale operation.

If the work is looking promising, there may be opportunities to investigate large scale activity in the Weymouth area.
